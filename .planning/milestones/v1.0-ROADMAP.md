## 1. Executive Summary
SkillSwap v1.0 delivers a fully functional, money-free community skill-exchange platform powered by time credits (1 hour of service = 1 credit). Across 11 planned phases and 21 execution plans, the application implements the complete end-to-end lifecycle: member onboarding with an initial 5-credit grant, multi-attribute catalog search, algorithmic and AI-semantic match recommendations, exchange requesting with balance validation, service scheduling with conflict detection, tamper-proof dual completion confirmation, atomic server-side wallet transfers in PostgreSQL transactions, verified community reputation reviews, and real-time in-app notifications with a unified 9-page responsive interface.

---

## 2. Key Accomplishments

1. **Foundations, Security & Time-Credit Wallet Grant (Phases 1 & 2)**:
   - Spring Boot 3 modular monolith and React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS architecture.
   - JWT authentication with secure password hashing (BCrypt strength 12).
   - Server-enforced automatic platform grant of 5 time credits upon registration.

2. **Skill Catalog & Discovery (Phases 3 & 4)**:
   - 36 curated standard skills across 6 domains (Technology, Languages, Academics, Trades, Arts, Wellness) seeded via Flyway with custom skill support.
   - Multi-criteria JPQL search and filtering by category, location, and star rating on the Explore Skills page.

3. **Intelligent Match Engine (Phase 5)**:
   - Weighted compatibility scoring formula: `0.4*Skill + 0.2*Location + 0.15*Availability + 0.15*Rating + 0.1*Experience`.
   - Semantic pairing logic bridging related domains (e.g. "Math" ↔ "Calculus") and reciprocal trade detection (+10% score boost).

4. **Exchange Request & Booking Coordination (Phases 6 & 7)**:
   - Dual-persona exchange management with segmented Incoming and Outgoing request dashboards.
   - Calendar booking workflow coordinating date, start time, and duration with server-side collision prevention.

5. **Dual Completion Verification & Atomic Ledger Settlement (Phases 8 & 9)**:
   - Independent two-party verification flags (`providerConfirmed`, `requesterConfirmed`) and dispute mediation triggers.
   - Atomic `@Transactional` credit settlement transferring 1 credit per hour from requester to provider upon dual confirmation.
   - Immutable audit ledger (`credit_transactions`) with idempotency guards and non-negative balance database constraints.

6. **Reputation & Real-Time In-App Notifications (Phases 10 & 11)**:
   - Verified 1–5 star reviews restricted strictly to completed exchange participants with atomic average rating recalculation.
   - Notification subsystem alerting users of exchange events, interactive `NotificationBell` navbar dropdown with unread badge, and polished 9-page responsive experience.
